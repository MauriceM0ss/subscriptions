Help

  • ＋ Add a subscription from the header — pick a category, a billing cycle (monthly or yearly) and the price. The other figures are worked out for you.
  • Monthly & yearly costs are derived from the one price you enter, so they always agree.
  • Total spent is estimated from the start date and billing cycle — how much this subscription has cost you so far.
  • Overview (top of the sidebar) totals your active spend and lists renewals coming up in the next {{ ' ' }}30 days.
  • Subscriptions are grouped by category in the sidebar; click one to see its details, then Edit or Delete.
  • Flip a subscription Enable / Disable from its page to pause it — great for services you only switch on for a month. It stops counting toward your totals, and “total spent” only ever counts the months it was actually on.
  • Change a price and the old one is kept: each subscription page shows its price history over time.
